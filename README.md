# 🔬 Enterprise Research Assistant — Research Copilot

Enterprise Research Assistant is an AI-powered research intelligence platform designed to help users explore, analyze, and synthesize information from complex research documents.

The platform uses Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to transform uploaded documents into a searchable knowledge base. Users can interact with their research through natural-language questions and receive context-aware responses grounded in the source documents.

## ✨ Key Features

- 📚 Multi-document knowledge base
- 🔍 Semantic search and intelligent retrieval
- 💬 AI-powered Research Copilot
- 🧠 Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
- 🔗 Source-grounded responses
- 📑 Research paper analysis
- ⚖️ Cross-document comparison
- 🧩 Multi-document knowledge synthesis
- 📊 Research insight extraction
- 💾 Persistent vector-based knowledge retrieval

## 🎯 Example Research Use Case

For demonstration, the platform can be provided with multiple research papers related to Vision Transformers and medical imaging.

The Research Copilot can then answer questions such as:

- How does a Vision Transformer process images for classification?
- How are Vision Transformers applied in medical image analysis?
- Compare Vision Transformers and CNNs for medical imaging.
- What common findings appear across the research papers?
- What evidence supports a particular conclusion?

This demonstrates the platform's ability to move from simple information retrieval to multi-document research synthesis and comparative analysis.

## 🚀 Project Goal

The goal of Enterprise Research Assistant is to reduce the time required to manually search and analyze large collections of documents by providing an intelligent Research Copilot capable of retrieving relevant evidence and generating research-oriented, source-grounded responses.
